# SIWES Guide

Straight answers about SIWES for Miva Open University students.

## Who it helps

Students getting ready for, doing, or finishing their industrial training. Ask in your own words and get short, step-by-step help, in plain language, at any hour.

## What you get

- **The coordinator's own answer first.** If the question has been answered before, you read that answer, then tell us whether it helped.
- **A patient helper when it didn't.** One tap opens a friendly assistant that explains things in small steps.
- **Honest answers.** Each one says where it came from, and points you to the coordinator when we don't know.
- **No sign-up.** Open it and ask. Please don't type your matric number or other personal details.
- **Works on a phone**, in light or dark.

## What it isn't

It doesn't replace the SIWES coordinator or your school's official notices. The helper is AI-assisted and can be wrong, so check anything important with the coordinator.

## Stage

Early version, being tested with students.
