# 0003 No landing page: the chat is the first screen

Status: accepted (explicit owner instruction)

**Context.** The owner said not to build a landing page at all. The audience arrives with a task.

**Decision.** The app opens straight into the chat shell: topic sidebar, a short empty state with quick-start tiles, and the input. No hero, no marketing sections, no footer pitch. The privacy notice is a dialog reachable from the sidebar footer and the consent banner.

**Consequences.** Do not add a marketing route. First-run orientation lives in the empty state of the chat.
